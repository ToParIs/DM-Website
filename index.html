About Us

Structural Engineering Consulting Since 1991

DAHL MARZIN INC. is a structural engineering consulting firm established in 1991, specializing in residential, commercial and industrial buildings. Our team possesses deep practical expertise in steel, concrete, and timber structures.

We design all types of structures and provide optimal, practical consultation with cost-effective and dependable solutions to help our clients meet their construction budget and project timelines.
